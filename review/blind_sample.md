## 题目 2

Why is LOPID therapy contraindicated when a patient is also prescribed repaglinide?

**参考答案**

Co-administration of LOPID with repaglinide significantly increases the risk of severe hypoglycemia due to elevated repaglinide plasma concentrations, which can occur in patients with abnormal liver function as indicated by LOPID's liver-related side effects.
